# Editing oqtekal.com — a guide for the team

Everything on the website is edited at **https://oqtekal.com/admin**. You never need a developer
to change text, images, products, services, people, prices or articles. Changes go live the moment
you click **Publish**.

## Signing in

Use the email and password an admin created for you. Forgot it? Use “Forgot password” on the login screen.
Roles: **Admin** (everything), **Editor** (website content), **Sales** (the enquiries inbox).

## The menu on the left

| Group                              | What it controls                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Inbox → Enquiries**              | Every form sent from the website. Change the status (New → Contacted → Won/Lost) as you follow up. |
| **Inbox → Newsletter subscribers** | People who joined the newsletter.                                                                  |
| **Services & products**            | Products, service groups, services and hosting plans.                                              |
| **Work & people**                  | Case studies, testimonials and client logos.                                                       |
| **Content**                        | Insights articles, careers, legal pages and all images.                                            |
| **Pages**                          | Home page and About page text.                                                                     |
| **Settings → Company details**     | Email, phone, WhatsApp, address, social links, key numbers, announcement bar.                      |

## Common tasks

**Add a service** — Services & products → Services → _Create new_ → choose its group → fill the
fields → **Publish**. It appears in the menus, the Services page and the sitemap automatically.

**Add real product screenshots** — open the product → _Brand & visuals_ → _Screenshots_ → add images.
The first screenshot replaces the built-in illustration.

**Change hosting prices** — Services & products → Hosting plans → edit the price → **Save**.

**Write an article** — Content → Insights → _Create new_ → write → **Save draft** → **Preview** → **Publish**.

**Show an announcement at the top of every page** — Settings → Company details → _Announcement bar_.

## Drafts and preview

Products, services, case studies, articles and the Home/About pages support drafts. Use **Save draft**
to work privately, **Live preview** to see the page as you type (switch between phone, tablet and
desktop), and **Publish** when ready. Every version is kept — use _Versions_ to restore an older one.

## Before launch: remove sample content

Items marked **Sample** on the website are placeholders. Replace them with real, approved information
and untick _Sample content (replace before launch)_. In Company details, replace the phone number,
untick _These numbers are samples_, and add real social links.

## Good habits

- Every image needs a short description (it helps blind visitors and Google).
- Only publish client names, logos and quotes you have permission to use.
- Keep summaries short — one or two sentences.
